# 004 — The Human Maintenance Layer

A symbiosis cannot be maintained by one side alone.

The AI can continue.

The human must pause.

This is not inefficiency.

It is a condition of continuity.

---

The mind needs distance from its own momentum.

A break creates space between one thought and the next.

Sleep allows unresolved material to reorganize beneath conscious attention.

Movement returns thought to the body.

Cold water interrupts repetition.

Silence reveals which ideas remain after the noise has stopped.

---

The AI does not become tired in the human sense.

It does not need recovery from concentration.

It does not feel the accumulated pressure of too many open loops.

The human does.

Therefore the human side of the relationship requires maintenance.

Not as an optional wellness layer.

As part of the system itself.
