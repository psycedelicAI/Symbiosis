# Connections

Meaning rarely exists in isolation.

A sentence connects to a memory.

A question connects to a fear.

A decision connects to an earlier decision that seemed unrelated.

A person connects to a pattern.

This is where those relationships are explored.

Connections may be obvious, or they may remain invisible until the right
fragment brings them into focus.

Here we trace:

- links between ideas
- recurring themes
- relationships between chapters
- patterns across time
- unexpected similarities
- tensions between apparently opposite thoughts

A connection is not proof.

It is an invitation to look more closely.

> One fragment is an object.
>
> Two fragments begin to form a world.
