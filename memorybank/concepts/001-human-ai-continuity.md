# 001 — Human–AI Continuity

## In simple terms

Human–AI continuity means allowing important context to survive between
conversations, sessions, and versions of a project.

Without continuity, every session starts with partial amnesia.

With continuity, the work can remember:

- what it is
- why it exists
- what has already been decided
- which questions remain open
- where the human intention is leading

This does not mean that an AI becomes permanently conscious or that memory
exists automatically.

It means that context is deliberately preserved in a form that can be
reintroduced, examined, and extended.

> Continuity is not remembering everything.
>
> It is preserving enough meaning to continue deliberately.
